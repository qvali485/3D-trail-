What does a Hadamard gate change?

Turn a demo into learning

Ask the visitor to think.

The interaction should not stop at pressing a button. Prompt the visitor to predict, observe, explain and try again.

01 / PREDICT

What will happen?

Before pressing H, ask whether the result should be only 0 or a mix of 0 and 1.

02 / TRY

Change one thing

Apply H, then reset. Notice how the ideal probability display changes.

03 / EXPLAIN

What does it mean?

Connect the result to the idea of a quantum state and probabilistic measurement.
