The idea behind the experience

Make the invisible approachable.

Quantum systems can look like a black box from the outside. This experience helps visitors move from seeing equipment to understanding the parts, questions and skills behind it.

“Open access” should mean more than entering a room. It should open a path to understanding, experimenting and contributing.Experience design principle — editorial copy, not a quotation from Dr. Subramaniam.
