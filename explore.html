Layer 01

Cold environment

Superconducting quantum hardware often operates at very low temperatures. Cryogenic equipment removes heat and creates the environment needed for the device under test.

Challenge question: If the temperature is not reaching the expected level, what would you check first—sensor reading, cooling cycle, thermal connections, or a system alarm?

Layer 02

Device & connections

The sample or quantum device must be mounted and connected in a way that supports the intended experiment. Wiring and connectors carry signals while creating engineering constraints.

Challenge question: Why might a team care about cable routing, connector quality, thermal anchoring and the number of available signal lines?

Layer 03

Control, readout & software

Control electronics generate signals used to manipulate the device; readout electronics collect signals that reveal measurement outcomes. Software helps coordinate experiments, calibration and analysis.

Challenge question: If a circuit runs but its measurements look unexpected, what would you inspect before assuming the quantum theory is wrong?

The aim is not to pretend every visitor can repair the machine. The aim is to help every visitor see that complex technology becomes more approachable when its parts, purpose and questions are made visible.Experience design principle.
