Existing interactive learning

What already exists—and what this concept must add.

Interactive quantum learning and circuit simulators already exist. This concept should not claim to invent interactive quantum education. Its potential distinction is to connect a visitor's physical experience of the actual facility to the hardware layers, engineering questions and a guided first experiment.
